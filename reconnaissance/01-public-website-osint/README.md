# Public Website OSINT

Passive reconnaissance of publicly available information relating to the target organisation.

## Objective

Review publicly accessible website content to identify information that could contribute to an understanding of the organisation's public exposure.

## Method

Browser-based research was used to review publicly accessible pages and information. No intrusive testing or interaction with internal systems was performed.

## Areas Reviewed

- Public website pages
- Contact information
- Organisation information
- Publicly available case studies
- Public business information

## Findings

The assessment identified publicly available organisational information, including contact details, services and business information.

No direct technical vulnerabilities were identified during this activity. However, publicly available information could potentially assist threat actors with reconnaissance or social engineering.

## Security Considerations

Organisations should regularly review publicly available information and ensure that unnecessary information is not exposed.
